Text A (Traditional Legend – Sangkuriang & Dayang Sumbi): In ancient West Java, a powerful young
man named Sangkuriang fell in love with a beautiful woman, Dayang Sumbi, without realizing that she had
been his own mother!
When Dayang Sumbi discovered the scar on his forehead, she told him the truth,
but Sangkuriang refused to believe her. To prevent the forbidden marriage, Dayang Sumbi set an impossible
condition: Sangkuriang had to dam the Citarum River to create a vast lake and build a giant wooden boat before
dawn. Aided by forest spirits, Sangkuriang almost finished the boat at 04.00 a.m. In panic, Dayang Sumbi waved
her white silk shawl, pounded rice mortars (lesung), and made roosters crow so the spirits thought dawn had
arrived and fled. Enraged by his failure, Sangkuriang kicked the giant boat, which landed upside down and
became Mount Tangkuban Perahu (as shown on the left of Gambar 3.2).
Text B (Multiverse Fantasy – Andre in the Cyber-Legend Portal): While Andre was reading the digital book
of Timun Mas and the Green Giant in the school library using AR smart glasses (right side of Gambar 3.2),
a holographic portal pulled him into the story! Instead of a wicked monster, the Giant (Buto Ijo) was actually
a giant forest guardian whose valley had dried up because of a volcanic drought. Timun Mas said to Andre,
"Let’s use my four botanical capsules to restore the valley instead of fighting!" Together, they planted the golden
cucumber seeds, which grew into giant water-storing bamboo and lush fruit trees. The grateful Forest Guardian
promised to protect the village forever!

• Searching for Information: In the 1990s, students used to go to the town library and flip through thick

• Communication: Teenagers used to send handwritten letters via post office or queue at coin-operated
public payphones (telepon umum koin). If they wanted to send a short urgent message, people used a pager
(beeper).
• Entertainment & Music: Friends used to record their favorite songs from the radio onto magnetic cassette
tapes and rewind the tape using a wooden pencil when the ribbon got loose!
• Photos: Families used to use analog film cameras (36 exposures per roll) and had to wait days at a photo
studio to print the pictures before they could see the results.
