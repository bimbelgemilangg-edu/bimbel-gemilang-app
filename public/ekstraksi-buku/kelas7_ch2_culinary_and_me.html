Goal: How to Make Monita’s Special Homemade Fried Rice (Nasi Goreng Spesial)
Ingredients: 2 plates of cold cooked rice, 2 cloves of garlic, 3 shallots, 2 red chilies (optional), 1 egg, 50g diced
chicken breast, 2 tablespoons of sweet soy sauce (kecap manis), 1
2 teaspoon of salt, 2 tablespoons of cooking
oil, cucumber slices, and fried shallots.
Utensils: A mortar and pestle (cobek dan ulekan), a wok, a spatula, a knife, a cutting board, a stove, and two
serving plates.
Steps:
1. First, peel the garlic and shallots, then grind them together with the red chilies and salt using a mortar and
pestle until smooth.
2. Second, heat two tablespoons of cooking oil in a wok over medium heat.
3. Next, stir-fry the ground spice paste until fragrant, then push it to the side of the wok and crack the egg into
the pan. Scramble the egg and add the diced chicken until cooked.
4. Then, put the cold cooked rice into the wok and pour the sweet soy sauce over the rice.
5. After that, stir and toss everything evenly using a spatula for 3 minutes until the rice is well-coated and
smoky.
6. Finally, turn off the stove, transfer the fried rice onto serving plates, garnish with cucumber slices and fried
shallots, and serve warm!
