- Because he is speaking to a teacher in a formal school situation.
- Because he is angry with Mr. Rahmat.
- Because they are meeting at a birthday party at night.
PEMBAHASAN:
Galang berbicara kepada guru di sekolah formal, sehingga ungkapan sopan resminya adalah "Allow me to introduce myself, Sir.".
