Goal: How to Save Electrical Energy in Your Bedroom Every Day
Why It Matters: Generating electricity mostly burns fossil fuels, which produces carbon dioxide emissions.
Saving electricity at home reduces carbon emissions and lowers your family’s monthly electricity bill!
Steps:
1. First, open your bedroom curtains and windows in the morning so natural sunlight and fresh air enter the
room.
2. Second, turn off the study lamp and ceiling light as soon as the room is bright enough.
3. Next, unplug your smartphone charger, laptop adapter, and fan plug from the wall socket when they are not
in use (because plugged-in chargers still consume "vampire power" or standby electricity!).
4. Then, set your air conditioner (AC) to an eco-friendly temperature (24◦C −26◦C) or use a low-wattage fan,
and turn it off 30 minutes before leaving the room.
5. Finally, always switch off all lights and electronics before leaving your bedroom (as shown on the right of
Gambar 3.2)!

Goal: How to Save Electrical Energy in Your Bedroom Every Day
Why It Matters: Generating electricity mostly burns fossil fuels, which produces carbon dioxide emissions.
Saving electricity at home reduces carbon emissions and lowers your family’s monthly electricity bill!
Steps:
