• NOTICE 1 (At the School Computer Lab): "CAUTION: DO NOT CLICK UNKNOWN LINKS OR SHARE YOUR PASSWORD. LOG OUT OF YOUR ACCOUNT BEFORE LEAVING THE COMPUTER."
• NOTICE 2 (At the Science Laboratory): "WARNING: CORROSIVE CHEMICALS. WEAR GLOVES AND SAFETY GOGGLES AT ALL TIMES. WASH HANDS THOROUGHLY AFTER EXPERIMENT."
TEKS:
[Membandingkan Dua Notice] How do Notice 1 and Notice 2 differ in their safety focus?
OPSI:
- Notice 1 focuses on cybersecurity and data privacy, while Notice 2 focuses on physical safety from hazardous chemicals.
- Notice 1 is found at a swimming pool, while Notice 2 is found at a library.
- Both notices tell students to share their passwords with strangers.
- Notice 1 is for chemistry teachers, while Notice 2 is for computer programmers.
PEMBAHASAN:
Notice 1 berfokus pada keamanan digital (cybersecurity) di lab komputer, sedangkan Notice 2 berfokus pada keselamatan fisik dari zat kimia korosif di lab sains.
