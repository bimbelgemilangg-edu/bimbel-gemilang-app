Soal #1 (PG SEDERHANA): [Analisis Konteks Sosial & Preposisi Alamat – HOTS] Why does Galang say "I live on Jalan Belitung" while Andre says "I live at Jalan Merdeka No. 15"?

  • Because Galang forgets his street name in Kalimantan.
  • Because "on" is used for a street name without a house number, whereas "at" is used for a specific address with a house number.
  • Because "on" is only used in the morning and "at" is used at night.
  • Because Jalan Belitung is the name of a province, not a street.
B

Pembahasan: on + nama jalan; at + alamat bernomor
