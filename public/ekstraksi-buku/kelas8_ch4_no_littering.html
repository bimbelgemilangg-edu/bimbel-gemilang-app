Authentic Stimulus (Recount Text / Dialogue / Fable / Poster): Last Sunday morning, a group of junior high school volunteers in Banyuwangi conducted a coastal cleanup at
Muncar Beach after a heavy storm the night before. While they were collecting plastic bottles and styrofoam
along the shoreline, Galang spotted a young green sea turtle (Chelonia mydas) struggling near the rocks. Its
front flipper was tightly entangled in a discarded nylon fishing net and plastic six-pack rings. Because of the
tight plastic entanglement, the turtle could not swim back to the ocean. Immediately, the team leader called
a local marine conservation officer. Using safety scissors, they carefully cut away the plastic net, cleaned the
turtle’s flipper, and released it safely into the sea. That afternoon, the volunteers collected 45 sacks of plastic
waste so that no other marine animals would be harmed.

Soal #1 (PG SEDERHANA): [Tata Bahasa Kontekstual: Cause & Effect (because vs because of)] Why does the writer use "Because of" instead of "Because" in the sentence "Because of the tight plastic entanglement, the turtle could not swim back to the ocean"?

  • Because "the tight plastic entanglement" is a Noun Phrase without a finite verb, not a full S + V clause.
  • Because the sentence is in the Simple Future Tense.
  • Because "Because of" can only be used at the end of a question.
  • Because the sea turtle is an uncountable noun.
A

Pembahasan: Jawaban yang tepat adalah A (Because "the tight plastic entanglement" is a Noun Phrase without a finite verb, not a full S + V clause.). Penjelasan: Because of + Noun Phrase.
