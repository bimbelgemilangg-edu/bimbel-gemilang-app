Goal: How to Manage Household Waste Using the 3R Principle (Reduce, Reuse, Recycle)
Equipment: Three labeled bins: Green Bin (Organic Waste), Yellow Bin (Non-Organic Recyclables), and Red
Bin (Hazardous/B3 Waste), rubber gloves, and a compost bucket.
Steps:
1. First, wear rubber gloves to keep your hands clean and safe before handling household waste.
2. Second, collect all kitchen food scraps, vegetable stems, fruit peels, and dry garden leaves, then put them
into the Green Bin to be processed into organic garden compost.
3. Next, rinse and flatten empty plastic water bottles, shampoo bottles, aluminum cans, and cardboard boxes,
then place them into the Yellow Bin for the recycling bank (Bank Sampah).
4. Then, separate broken glass lamps, used batteries, and expired medicine bottles, and put them safely into
the Red Bin so they do not poison the soil or injure sanitation workers.
5. Finally, wash your hands with soap under running water and keep all bin lids closed tightly!
