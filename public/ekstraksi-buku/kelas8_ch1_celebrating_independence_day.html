Authentic Stimulus (Recount Text / Dialogue / Fable / Poster): Text 1 (Monita’s Parade Experience): On August 17th morning, I joined the SMP Merdeka Cultural Parade. All
seventh and eighth graders (1) ... traditional costumes from different provinces. I wore a traditional Baju Bodo
from South Sulawesi, while Pipit wore a Minangkabau costume with a horn-shaped headdress (Tingkuluak).
First, we gathered at the school yard at 07.30 a.m.
Next, the marching band led the parade along Jalan
Merdeka. Many local residents stood along the sidewalk, waved small red-and-white flags, and took photos of
us. At 10.30 a.m., we returned to the school hall where the principal announced the "Best Traditional Costume"
winner. To my surprise, Pipit won first place!
Text 2 (Monita’s Card for Pipit): "Dear Pipit, Congratulations on winning 1st Place in the Best Traditional
Costume Contest at SMP Merdeka’s Independence Day Parade! You looked stunning and graceful in your
Minangkabau costume. Keep shining and preserving our Indonesian culture! Best wishes, Monita."

Last year, SMP Merdeka celebrated Indonesia’s 78th Independence Day with various traditional games in the
schoolyard. Each class sent representatives to compete in the Kerupuk Race, Marble-in-Spoon Race, Tan-
dem Clogs (Bakiak) Race, and Sack Race.
First, Andre joined the Kerupuk Race. His hands were tied behind his back while he jumped to bite a cracker
hanging on a string. He ate his cracker very fast and won second place! Next, Monita participated in the
Marble-in-Spoon Race. She walked carefully while holding a spoon with a glass marble in her mouth. Halfway
to the finish line, the wind blew strongly, but her marble (9) ... fall to the ground. She finished first!
After that, Galang, Made, and Riko joined the three-person Bakiak Race. At the start, they lost their balance
and almost fell because they did not step at the same time. Then, Galang shouted, "Left, right, left, right!" to
synchronize their footsteps. Working together in harmony, they overtook two other teams and crossed the finish
line first!
