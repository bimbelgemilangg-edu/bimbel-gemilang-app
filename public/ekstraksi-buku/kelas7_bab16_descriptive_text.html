TEXT 1 (BOROBUDUR TEMPLE): Borobudur is the world’s largest Buddhist temple, located in Magelang, Central Java. Built in the 9th century during the Syailendra Dynasty, the monument consists of nine stacked platforms—six square and three circular—topped by a magnificent central dome. It is decorated with 2,672 relief panels and 504 Buddha statues, built from dark gray volcanic andesite stone without using any cement.

TEXT 2 (PRAMBANAN TEMPLE): Prambanan is the largest Hindu temple complex in Indonesia, located on the border between Central Java and Yogyakarta. Also built in the 9th century, Prambanan is famous for its tall, pointed architecture. Its three main shrines are dedicated to Trimurti (Brahma, Vishnu, and Shiva), with the central Shiva temple rising 47 meters high. Its stone reliefs tell the epic story of Ramayana.
