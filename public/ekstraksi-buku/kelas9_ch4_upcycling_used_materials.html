Authentic Stimulus (Report Text / Travel Recount / Narrative Legend / Procedure & Infographics): How to Make an Eco-Friendly Denim Desk Organizer:
Materials: 1 clean tin can, a piece of old blue jeans (denim fabric), multipurpose glue, scissors, and batik
ribbon.
Steps: (1) Wash and dry the used tin can thoroughly, ensuring there are no sharp edges on the rim. (2)
Measure and cut the old denim fabric to fit around the tin can. (3) Apply glue evenly onto the outside of the
can and wrap the denim fabric tightly around it. (4) Glue a strip of traditional batik ribbon around the top and
bottom borders for decoration. Your desk organizer is ready to display at the SMP Merdeka Charity Shop
(Price: Rp15.000 each; 100% of sales will be donated to flood victims)!
