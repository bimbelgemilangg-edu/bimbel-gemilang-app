Banyuwangi, November 5th, 2026
Dear Cousin Tamara in Bandung,
How are you doing? I hope you and Aunt Sari are doing great! I am writing this letter to tell you about my
school field trip to Bangsring Underwater Conservation in Banyuwangi last weekend. Have you ever visited
a floating shark conservation house (Rumah Apung)? It was amazing!
We arrived at Bangsring Beach at 08.30 a.m. First, a marine biologist explained how local fishermen trans-
formed damaged coral reefs into a protected marine conservation zone.
Next, we put on our life jackets,
snorkeling masks, and fins, and took a short boat ride to the Floating House. While I was floating on the
crystal-clear water, hundreds of blue-and-yellow damselfish (ikan betok laut) swam around my hands! After
lunch, we planted baby coral fragments onto underwater iron frames to help restore the reef. I have attached
three photos for you. You must visit Banyuwangi next holiday!
Warmest love,
Monita
