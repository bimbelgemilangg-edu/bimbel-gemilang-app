Authentic Stimulus (Recount Text / Dialogue / Fable / Poster): One morning, a lone elephant wandered into a thick forest looking for friends. First, she met a monkey on a
branch. "Will you be my friend, Monkey?" asked the elephant. The monkey replied arrogantly, "You are too
big and you cannot swing on trees like me, so I cannot be your friend!" Next, the elephant met a rabbit and
a frog, but both rejected her because she could not burrow in small holes or leap across lily pads. While the
elephant (1) ... sadly under a banyan tree, a fierce tiger suddenly entered the forest and tried to attack the small
animals. Seeing her friends in danger, the brave elephant trumpeted loudly and stomped the ground until the
terrified tiger ran away. The monkey, rabbit, and frog felt ashamed of their earlier words. They apologized to the
elephant and said, "You are just the right size to be our best friend and protector!"

Soal #1 (PG SEDERHANA): [Tata Bahasa Kontekstual: Past Continuous Tense with While] What is the correct verb form to complete blank (1) in the sentence "While the elephant (1) ... sadly under a banyan tree, a fierce tiger suddenly entered the forest"?

  • walks
  • was resting
  • is resting
  • were resting
B

Pembahasan: Jawaban yang tepat adalah B (was resting). Penjelasan: was resting.

Fable A (The Lion and the Mouse): One hot afternoon, a mighty Lion was sleeping peacefully in his den when
a tiny Mouse accidentally ran across his nose. The Lion woke up angrily and caught the Mouse with his huge
paw. "Please spare my life, O King! Someday I will surely repay your kindness," begged the trembling Mouse.
The Lion laughed at the idea that such a small creature could ever help him, but he felt pity and let the Mouse
go. A few days later, while the Lion (9) ... for food in the forest, he fell into a hunter’s strong rope net. He roared
in despair because he could not break free. Hearing the roar, the little Mouse ran to the spot, gnawed through
the thick ropes with his sharp teeth, and set the Lion free!
Fable B (The Ant and the Dove): One sunny morning, a thirsty Ant walked down to a fast-flowing stream to
drink. Suddenly, a strong wave swept the Ant into the water, and he was about to drown. A kind white Dove
sitting on a tree branch overhanging the river saw the Ant in trouble. Quickly, the Dove plucked a green leaf
and dropped it into the stream near the struggling Ant. The Ant climbed onto the leaf and floated safely to the
riverbank. Later that afternoon, a hunter came and aimed his net at the Dove. Seeing this, the grateful Ant bit
the hunter’s heel sharply. Startled, the hunter dropped his net, and the Dove flew away safely!

Authentic Stimulus (Recount Text / Dialogue / Fable / Poster): Read the Story of Kindness at SMP Merdeka below for questions number 16 to 25 (Unit 3: Kindness and
Friendship in Daily Life)!
Last Monday morning, it was raining heavily when Bagas arrived at SMP Merdeka. While he was rushing
up the corridor stairs with a stack of twenty science workbooks, his shoe slipped on a wet tile! All twenty
workbooks scattered across the floor, and Bagas hurt his ankle. Two older students walked past and laughed
at him, which made Bagas feel embarrassed and sad. Fortunately, Monita and Galang were walking behind
him. Without hesitation, Galang helped Bagas stand up and accompanied him to the School Clinic (UKS), while
Monita picked up all the workbooks, wiped the wet covers with her tissue, and delivered them to the science
teacher’s desk. Bagas felt deeply grateful and said, "Thank you so much, Galang and Monita. Your kindness
turned my terrible morning into a warm one!"
