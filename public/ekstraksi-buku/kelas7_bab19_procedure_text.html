How to Make a Self-Watering Plant Pot from a Used Plastic Bottle

Materials: A 1.5-liter used plastic bottle, a cutter/scissors, a nail, a thick cotton string (20 cm), water, soil, and a small plant seedling.

Steps:
1. Step 1: Cut the plastic bottle in half using a cutter and scissors so you have a bottom part and a top part.
2. Step 2: Make a small hole in the bottle cap using a nail, then thread the cotton string through the hole.
3. Step 3: Fill the bottom half of the bottle with water, then place the top half upside down (inverted) into the bottom half so the cotton string touches the water.
4. Step 4: Put fertile soil into the inverted top half using a small trowel and plant your seedling. The cotton string will absorb water up into the soil automatically!
