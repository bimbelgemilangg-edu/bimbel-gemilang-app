Campus Guide of SMP Merdeka:
When you enter SMP Merdeka through the Main Gate on the south side, the Security Post is on your left
and the Parking Area is on your right. Walking straight ahead, you will reach the Main Courtyard with a tall
flagpole in the center. Directly behind the flagpole (on the north side) is the Principal’s Office, which is flanked
by the Teachers’ Room on the west and the Administration Office (TU) on the east. Along the west wing,
there are Class VII-A, Class VII-B, Class VII-C, and the School Clinic (UKS). Along the east wing, there are
the Library, the Language Lab, and the Science Lab. At the back of the school (north-east corner), you will
find the Canteen beside the School Mosque.
Notice Inside the School Library:
"LIBRARY RULES: (1) PLEASE KEEP SILENT – DO NOT DISTURB OTHER READERS. (2) RETURN BOOKS
TO THE BORROWING DESK ON TIME. (3) NO FOOD, CANDY, OR DRINKS INSIDE THE READING AREA."
